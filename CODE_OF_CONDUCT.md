# Code of conduct

ArgBridge aims to be a welcoming place to discuss engineering problems. Treat contributors with respect regardless of experience, identity, background or employer.

- Be specific, constructive and willing to revise your view when evidence changes.
- Critique code and claims rather than people. Respect reasonable disagreement.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, disclose another person's private information, or send unwanted sexual content.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time. Participation does not imply a support obligation or a response-time guarantee.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, reviews and project-related interactions. Maintainers may remove content, limit interaction or exclude participants to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and consider the safety and privacy of affected people.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner through a private contact channel they have explicitly published. If no private channel is available, open a minimal request for a private reporting channel without naming affected people or including sensitive details. Do not publish incident details in an issue. A maintainer involved in a report should not be its sole decision-maker; if no independent maintainer is available, that limitation should be disclosed.

This is a project-specific policy. It does not promise confidential case management infrastructure that the project does not yet have.
